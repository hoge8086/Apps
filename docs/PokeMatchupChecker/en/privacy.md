# Privacy Policy

Kenta Hayakawa (the "Operator") handles user information in the smartphone application PokeMatchupChecker (the "App") as described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information Collected or Transmitted

The App does not provide the Operator with any feature that directly collects a user's name, postal address, telephone number, email address, or credit card number. However, information may be collected from or transmitted by the user's device in connection with the following third-party services.

### Google AdMob

For advertising, measurement of advertising effectiveness, prevention of fraud and abuse, and service improvement, information such as advertising identifiers, IP addresses, device, operating system and app information, ad impressions and interactions, diagnostic information, and other information specified by Google may be transmitted to Google LLC. Ad personalization may be restricted depending on the device settings, consent status, and applicable law.

- [Google Policies for Advertising](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads?hl=en)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en)

### App Store and StoreKit

Apple Inc. handles product identifiers, purchase status, transaction information, and other information required by Apple to process payments for the purchase of a Premium Plan, verification of subscription status, and restoration of purchases. The Operator does not obtain credit card numbers or other payment-method details.

- [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## 2. Purposes of Use

Information collected or transmitted is used for the following purposes:

- Serving advertisements, measuring advertising effectiveness, and preventing fraud and abuse
- Processing subscription purchases, verifying subscription status, providing features, and restoring purchases
- Maintaining the quality of the App, diagnosing failures, and improving the App
- Responding to conduct that violates applicable law or the Terms of Use

## 3. Disclosure to Third Parties and External Transmission

The information described above may be transmitted to Google LLC or Apple Inc. to the extent necessary to provide the relevant third-party service and will be handled in accordance with each company's privacy policy. Except where required by law, the Operator will not disclose user information obtained by the Operator to any other third party without the user's consent.

## 4. User Controls

Users may restrict advertising identifiers and personalized advertising through the iOS privacy settings and other applicable controls. To stop information from being transmitted by the App, uninstall the App. For requests concerning purchase history and payment information held by the App Store, please contact Apple.

## 5. Use by Minors

Minors must obtain consent from a parent or legal guardian before using the App.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

The Operator may revise this Privacy Policy in response to changes in applicable law, the App, or the services used by the App. Material changes will be announced on this page or by another appropriate method.
